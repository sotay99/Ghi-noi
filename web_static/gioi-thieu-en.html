Flowrite

Speak your ideas, type to fix them on the fly — no waiting, no conflicts, no losing your train of thought.

Voice and typing have always forced you to pick one: turn on dictation and the keyboard goes quiet, or type and stay silent. Flowrite breaks that rule — you speak and type at the same time, with no clashing or overwriting, plus multi-note organization, built-in AI, and sync across every device.

I. Speak and type at the same time

Most speech-to-text apps force you to pick ONE of the two: either speak, or type. Flowrite lets both happen naturally interleaved — say a long sentence, then reach over and type in a word or a number right away — no need to turn off the mic, no worry about voice and typed characters clashing and breaking the sentence.

II. Manage many notes, switch files in one beat

Most other note apps overlook this

Notes are organized into nested folders, like a real folder tree — not one long, disorganized list. And while editing a file, you can jump straight to the next file in the same folder with the "Previous file/Next file" button right on the editing screen.

  • Switching to another note does NOT mean going back to the folder screen and reopening each one — just one button, right where you are.
  • The last file wraps back to the first (and vice versa) — never "stuck at the edge".
  • Quick search inside a folder, and an option to auto-open the right note on every startup.

III. Type faster with the clipboard history, quick dictionary, spell-check

Three small helpers, a big payoff

Repeated bits of text — signatures, greetings, frequent phrases — don't need retyping every time. And small typos don't need you to scan every word to fix them.

  • Clipboard history: keeps your last 10 cut/copy/paste actions, pin any line to keep it longer — paste it back exactly any time, no retyping.
  • Quick-entry dictionary: a 26-row table of your frequent snippets, one tap pastes it straight into the text — much faster than typing repeated phrases by hand.
  • Spelling dictionary: automatically spots and suggests fixes for your usual typos, keeping the text clean without stopping to proofread.

IV. A built-in AI assistant

You never leave the app

You don't need to leave Flowrite to use AI — every AI feature lives right in the toolbar, working directly on the text you are editing.

  • Check spelling, summarize, or rewrite the whole text or just a selected paragraph.
  • Chat right inside the app to brainstorm or ask about your writing.
  • Read a photo or an audio file and turn it into text automatically.

V. Sync across devices

Sign in with a Google account (entirely optional) to open that same note on another device — phone, computer, any browser sees the exact same data.

VI. Quick notes from the home screen (Android)

Android only — iPhone/iPad support coming later

Place a Flowrite widget right on your Android phone/tablet home screen — jot down a quick thought without opening the app.

VII. Tips to go even faster

The app itself is only half the story — the other half is your own habits: add an external keyboard, pick the right input method (Telex/VNI for Vietnamese), learn 10-finger touch typing, and use an external mic for clearer voice input.
